Before you buy

Clear expectations

Are these GTA VI products?

No. High End Studios is independent of Rockstar Games. These are original community planning and creative tools. We do not sell game files, official assets or access to GTA VI.

Will a kit build my server for me?

No. You receive the files described in each listing and adapt them for your own community. The Discord kit includes setup instructions and editable text and CSV files, but requires you to configure your server.

What license comes with a kit?

The products are intended for one community. Read the license and full delivery list on the individual marketplace page before checkout.
